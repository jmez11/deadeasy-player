DeadEasy Player

Watch 3D in real 3D.

You want to watch 3D in real 3D? This does that.

DeadEasy Player plays your 3D movies the way they were meant to be seen — side-by-side and over-under videos, in actual 3D, right in your headset.

What it does

  • Plays 3D movies in genuine stereoscopic 3D — not a flat picture, the real thing
  • Works with files on your device or over your home network
  • Other apps can hand videos off to it — use it as the external player for apps like Plezy
  • Subtitle and audio track selection, simple on-screen controls
